The format

Same rules. Paper format.

Each puzzle presents a grid of letters — every single one belonging to the hidden word. No noise, no red herrings. The satisfaction of finding the path is identical whether you're on a phone screen or a printed page.

Five chapters per book means the difficulty climbs naturally. Chapter one is accessible for any solver. Chapter five is a genuine challenge.
