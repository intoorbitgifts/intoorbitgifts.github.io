How it works

Three steps. That's it.

01

Open the grid

Every day a new grid appears containing a hidden word. Every tile in that grid is a letter from that word — and only that word. No red herrings.

02

Connect the letters

Tap adjacent tiles to spell out the hidden word in any direction — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal. Tap back to undo a letter.

03

Come back tomorrow

One puzzle per day. Keep your streak alive. Use your daily hint if you're stuck — but it'll cost you your no-hint streak.

Game modes

Two ways to play

Daily

The daily puzzle

One word hidden in a fresh grid every day. Simple, satisfying, and over in under a minute if you're sharp.

  • New puzzle every day
  • Streak tracking and honours
  • One hint per puzzle
  • Past puzzles to catch up on
  • 8 colour themes to unlock

Pressure Mode

Race the clock

50 levels, one timer. Find each word before time runs out — each correct answer earns bonus seconds. Speed and combo bonuses push your score higher.

  • 50 levels per season
  • Speed bonus for fast solves
  • Combo bonus for back-to-back words
  • 10 themed seasons available
  • Season 1 free — seasons 2–10 unlockable
