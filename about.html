About Into Orbit

Small studio.
Deliberate work.

Into Orbit is an independent publisher of puzzle books and digital games — built on the belief that well-made things are worth making.

Independent by choice

Into Orbit was founded to create the kind of products that feel considered — where the rules are clear, the design is accessible, and the experience is worth returning to.

Working independently means every decision is intentional. There are no committees, no trend-chasing, and no compromise on quality. Just ideas that are worth executing, executed properly.

Our work spans mobile puzzle games and puzzle books. Different formats, same principles: restraint, clarity, and genuine care for the people using them.

How we work

What we believe in

01

Simplicity first

The best products are the ones that don't need explaining. We strip away everything unnecessary until only the good stuff remains.

02

Designed to last

We're not interested in one-time experiences. Everything we make is built to be returned to — daily puzzles, books you pick up again, games that grow with you.

03

Accessible always

Our products are made for everyone — not just people who consider themselves "gamers" or "puzzle people". Clear design and broad appeal are never at odds.
