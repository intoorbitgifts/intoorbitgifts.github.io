Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026  ·  Into Orbit Publishing

The short version: We don't collect your personal data through the NODO or FluiDiary apps. Both apps store your data locally on your device. This website uses no tracking cookies. We will never sell your data to anyone, ever.

1. Who we are

Into Orbit Publishing ("Into Orbit", "we", "us", "our") operates the website intoorbit.co.uk and publishes the NODO and FluiDiary mobile applications. We are based in the United Kingdom.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@intoorbit.co.uk.

2. What data we collect — and what we don't

NODO App

The NODO app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. All game data — including streaks, statistics, completed puzzles, theme preferences, and notification settings — is stored locally on your device only.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools within the NODO app that would transmit data to us or third parties.

FluiDiary App

FluiDiary is a private journalling app. Your diary entries — including any text, timestamps, and audio recordings you create — are stored locally on your device only. Into Orbit Publishing does not collect, receive, transmit, or have any access to the contents of your diary.

FluiDiary requests access to your device's microphone for two separate features:

  • Voice dictation — lets you speak an entry instead of typing it. Dictation is transcribed using your device's own built-in speech recognition service. Depending on your device and its settings, this transcription may be processed by your device manufacturer or Google rather than entirely on-device. Into Orbit Publishing does not receive or store the audio used for dictation.
  • Voice memo recordings — lets you attach a short audio recording to an entry. These recordings are captured and stored entirely on your device. They are never transmitted to Into Orbit Publishing or any third party by the app.

If you enable FluiDiary's optional biometric lock (fingerprint or face unlock), authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Into Orbit Publishing never receives, sees, or stores any biometric data — the app is only told whether your device confirmed your identity, nothing more.

FluiDiary supports Android's built-in Auto Backup feature. If enabled on your device, your entries and recordings may be included in your device's backup to your own personal Google account. This backup is between you and Google; Into Orbit Publishing has no access to it. FluiDiary also offers a manual export feature (Settings → Backup) that creates a file of your entries for you to save or share wherever you choose — the destination is entirely your decision.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools within the FluiDiary app that would transmit data to us or third parties.

In-App Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase within NODO, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase verification is handled directly between your device and the relevant platform. Their privacy policies apply to those transactions:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

This Website

The intoorbit.co.uk website does not use tracking cookies, analytics platforms, or advertising pixels. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from visitors to this site.

If you contact us via email, we will use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry. We will not add you to mailing lists or share your contact details with third parties.

3. Children's privacy

NODO is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Because NODO stores all data locally and collects no personal data at all, it is suitable for use by players of all ages.

FluiDiary is a general-purpose journalling tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 through FluiDiary.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information via email or any other channel, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

4. Data storage and security

As noted above, all NODO game data and all FluiDiary entries and recordings are stored locally on your device using your device's standard storage mechanisms. We have no server-side access to this data. If you delete either app, your data is removed from your device (subject to any backup you've separately created via Android Auto Backup or FluiDiary's manual export feature). We recommend using each app's in-app Export feature to save a backup before uninstalling.

5. Your rights under UK GDPR

Under UK data protection law, you have the following rights regarding any personal data we might hold:

  • Right of access — you can ask what data we hold about you
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete any data we hold
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to restrict processing — you can ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — you can ask for a copy of your data

In practice, since neither the NODO nor FluiDiary app stores data on our servers, most of these rights apply only to any emails you may have sent us. To exercise any right, contact us at hello@intoorbit.co.uk.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data rights have been infringed.

6. Third-party services

The only third-party services involved in NODO's and FluiDiary's distribution are Google Play and the Apple App Store, which handle app delivery and purchase processing under their own privacy policies (linked above). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these platforms.

FluiDiary's voice dictation feature relies on your device's built-in speech recognition service, which — depending on your device — may be provided by Google or your device manufacturer. Android's Auto Backup feature, if enabled on your device, is provided by Google. Neither of these is controlled by Into Orbit Publishing, and we encourage you to review Google's own privacy policy for details on how they handle data processed through your device's operating system.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. Neither the NODO nor FluiDiary app uses cookies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our products after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, please contact us at hello@intoorbit.co.uk. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
