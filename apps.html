NODO - Wordsearch with a Twist and a Turn

NODO is the only word puzzle where the grid contains exclusively letters from the hidden word — no noise, no decoys. Connect them in any direction to find the answer. One puzzle each day. Simple, fair, and satisfying.

Pressure Mode adds a timer and 50 levels across 10 themed seasons for when you want a real challenge. Eight colour themes to unlock through play. No ads.
